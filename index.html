    Scoring method

    Only entered measurements are scored. An in-spec parameter receives 100. For a maximum limit, the score is min(100, limit / measured × 100); for a minimum, min(100, measured / limit × 100); an acceptable range is 100 inside the range and declines with distance outside it. The index is the unweighted mean of available parameter scores, and its coverage is shown alongside it. It is a transparent educational summary, not certification.

    LAB NOTES / EDUCATION

    Understanding biodiesel

    A compact reference layer for a project demonstration.

    01

    What is biodiesel?

    A fuel composed principally of fatty-acid alkyl esters, commonly FAME when methanol is used.

    02

    Transesterification

    Triglycerides react with an alcohol, usually in the presence of a catalyst, to form esters and glycerol.

    03

    Why FFA matters

    Free fatty acids can consume base and promote soap formation, complicating phase separation.

    04

    Why quality matters

    Fuel properties such as viscosity, residual alcohol, glycerol and water influence safety, performance and handling.

    05

    Biodiesel vs diesel

    Biodiesel can offer renewable carbon and improved lubricity; cold-flow behavior and compatibility need assessment.

    06

    What affects yield?

    Alcohol ratio, catalyst, moisture, FFA, mixing, temperature, time and separation all influence observed recovery.

    !

    LABORATORY SAFETY

    Methanol is toxic and flammable. Base catalysts are corrosive.

    Use approved institutional SOPs, appropriate PPE and engineering controls such as a fume hood. Manage spills and chemical waste through approved procedures; this educational tool does not provide handling instructions.
